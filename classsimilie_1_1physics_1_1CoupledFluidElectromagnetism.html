Detailed Description

template<PhysicsConcept FluidPhysics, PhysicsConcept ElectromagneticPhysics, EnergyConvention Energy = EnergyConvention::Fluid>
class similie::physics::CoupledFluidElectromagnetism< FluidPhysics, ElectromagneticPhysics, Energy >

Assemble fluid and electric, magnetic, or full Maxwell sectors with shared exchange laws.

The state contains the two physical sectors; the stateless interactions own no new degrees of freedom. get<0>() is the fluid, get<1>() is the field model, and get<2/3>() are the generated electric/magnetic coupling objects.

The caller computes spatial derivatives with exterior operators. In particular, it supplies one reduced current to both the Maxwell update and the force/power evaluation, and uses that current for the charge divergence. Constitutive current laws and material parameters belong to the field model.

For compressible flow, Fluid stores thermal+kinetic energy and adds J.E to its rate. FluidAndField stores that energy plus W(D,B), subtracts W when evaluating fluid primitives, and adds E cross H to the energy flux instead. Its rate receives no internal exchange source. Joule heat must not be added again. The field_energy_source diagnostic is already represented by -J in the displacement update; it is not an additional field evolution equation.

For incompressible flow, transport includes force/density before taking its curl. The caller supplies that complete curl to the vorticity update; no thermal or total-energy variable is evolved in this formulation.
