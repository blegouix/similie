Hodge Laplacian assembled from independently selected Hodge-star policies.

Important
This operator and documentation is fully AI-generated. DiscreteHodgeStar evaluates geometry during application. Wrapping it in StagedOperator freezes Hodge coefficients at construction. The same incidence operators, intermediate cochains and boundary closures are used in both cases. Policies have the DiscreteHodgeStar template signature and pointwise API. Stateful policies are constructed with (execution space, metric, positions); stateless policies may be default-constructed.
