Assemble any local differential operator with a stencil-producing value().

Important
This operator and documentation is fully AI-generated. The factory adapts the operator-specific value() arguments to (operator, input, rules, element, output_component). InputDomains supplies donor and boundary rules; OutputDomains supplies globally numbered rows. Coordinates selects absolute samples (Coboundary) or offsets plus one (the scalar Codifferential stencil). Rows and columns need not have equal counts. The returned rhs is the negative affine contribution of the rules. InputRole selects the primal, flux or geometry sampling policies. Every output row must have a unique owner. Empty rows retain a unit scaling. The factory must be copyable and callable in device kernels. For prescribed nodal constraints, use the lower-level detail::assemble_differential_rows and prescribe rows before finalizing, as the scalar Laplacian adapter does.
