Detailed Description

template<std::size_t FibreRank, class... SpatialIndex>
class sil::exterior::VertexReconstructedDerivative< FibreRank, SpatialIndex >

Reconstruct derivatives of nodal, flat-bundle-valued zero-cochains at the vertices of one cubical cell.

FibreRank is independent of spatial dimension.

Important
This operator and documentation is fully AI-generated. The canonical covariant coboundary supplies incidence; Reconstruction maps each one-cochain into the physical frame. Coefficients are cached for every nodal basis function. This evaluator supports vertex sampling only; it makes no assumption about the quadrature rule used by a variational assembler. measures contains the positive Euclidean volume Jacobian at each vertex, without quadrature weights. Components are packed fibre first, then axis.
