Detailed Description

template<class Direction, class Cochain, class Position, class GeometryRules, class BoundaryFluxRules>
struct sil::exterior::DualHodgeCochain< Direction, Cochain, Position, GeometryRules, BoundaryFluxRules >

Lazy dual cochain obtained by applying the Hodge star to a primal one-cochain.

Important
This operator and documentation is fully AI-generated. The input cochain can be stored or lazily evaluated. Geometry samples cross interfaces through their rules. The local primal Hodge pairing reproduces constant forms using the full cell Jacobian. Cross-axis terms use averages of parallel primal edges. A symmetric projected diagonal pairing controls unresolved edge modes while vanishing on constant physical forms, including on warped cells, recovering axial DEC weights on orthogonal grids. Incident physical cells supply the dual-face measure, including half faces at walls. At physical upper faces the outgoing dual sample comes from the physical flux policy, including the zero wall law.
