Detailed Description

template<class Field, class Rules, class Fixed = misc::SignedDiscreteElement<>>
struct sil::tensor::TensorWithExtrapolationRules< Field, Rules, Fixed >

Lazy tensor sampling on its stored domain and its extrapolated exterior.

Important
This operator and documentation is fully AI-generated. Coordinates remain signed until the face rules resolve them to a stored sample. value(domain, element) returns spatial basis weights without reading field values. This view performs no differentiation. Slices retain fixed coordinates and the original policies, so donor mappings and corner weights remain valid even when a slice fixes an exterior coordinate. Computed samples are read-only values; the caller updates the stored field through its Tensor. The view borrows tensor allocations through its field and rule handles; their owners must outlive it and synchronize updates before sampling.
