Detailed Description

Constant-density incompressible Navier-Stokes in vorticity form.

dt omega = curl(u cross omega) + (mu/rho) Delta omega, -Delta A=omega, div A=0, u=curl A. The vector potential has zero mean on a periodic domain; any harmonic mean velocity must be supplied separately. For pressure recovery, dt u = u cross omega - grad(Pi) + (mu/rho) Delta u, Pi=p/rho+|u|^2/2. Gradients, curl, divergence, and the physical (negative semidefinite) Laplacian are evaluated with the exterior module by the caller. The pressure solve is a numerical constraint solve, not potential-flow physics.
